    FAQ

    LinkedIn rewriting questions

    Will VoiceDraft add a personal story?

    No. The editor is instructed not to invent anecdotes, results, or experiences that are not present in your draft.

    Does it add hashtags?

    It does not add hashtags unless the source draft already contains them.

    Can I make the post shorter?

    Yes. After the first rewrite, use the Shorter adjustment without writing another prompt.
