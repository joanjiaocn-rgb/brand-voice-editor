    FAQ

    Email rewriting questions

    What kinds of emails can I rewrite?

    VoiceDraft is designed for client, prospect, partnership, and everyday professional emails up to 5,000 characters.

    Will it invent details?

    It is instructed not to invent details and checks selected names, numbers, dates, links, and contact information. Automated checks can miss context, so review business-critical messages.

    Can it use my normal writing style?

    Yes. Build a Voice Profile from representative writing and the editor will apply those observable preferences to later drafts in this browser.
