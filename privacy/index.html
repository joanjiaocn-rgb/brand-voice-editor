Privacy Policy

MVP notice: This draft must be reviewed with the final operator identity, contact details, analytics configuration, model provider settings, and retention policy before commercial launch.

What this policy covers

This policy explains how the VoiceDraft MVP processes information when you use the public website, rewriting tool, or local Voice Profile.

Writing you submit

Drafts, context, and writing samples are sent to the configured Cloudflare Workers AI service only to provide the requested rewrite or profile analysis. The application is designed not to persist raw draft, output, or sample text in its own database during this MVP. Service providers may process operational data according to their own agreements and configured retention settings.

Voice Profile storage

The MVP saves your derived Voice Profile in your browser's local storage. It is not an account and does not sync across devices. You can delete it from the Voice Profile page or by clearing site data in your browser.

Technical data

Hosting and security providers may process IP addresses, request timestamps, device and browser details, and error information needed to operate and protect the service. Application logs are designed not to include raw writing.

Analytics

VoiceDraft uses optional Google Analytics 4 to measure page visits and aggregate product actions such as starting a rewrite, completing a rewrite, copying a result, and managing a Voice Profile. Analytics is disabled until you choose “Allow analytics” in the preference notice. If you allow it, Google may set analytics cookies and receive the page path, page title, writing mode, and non-content status properties. We do not send drafts, rewrites, writing samples, or freeform context to analytics. See Google’s privacy policy for Google’s handling of analytics data.

How information is used

  • Provide the requested rewrite or profile analysis.
  • Prevent abuse and maintain reliability.
  • Measure aggregate product actions without sending writing content to analytics.
  • Meet legal obligations and enforce the Terms.

Retention and deletion

The local Voice Profile remains until you delete it or clear browser storage. Final production log retention periods are still to be confirmed. A commercial release must publish accurate periods and a working privacy contact.

Your choices

Do not submit secrets, regulated data, or information you are not authorized to share. You can delete your local profile at any time. Additional access, correction, and deletion rights may apply based on your location.

Contact

The operator's legal name and privacy contact are pending before commercial launch.
