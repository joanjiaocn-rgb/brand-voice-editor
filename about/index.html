About VoiceDraft

A practical editing desk for work writing

VoiceDraft is an AI humanizer for professional writing. It helps independent professionals turn AI-assisted emails and LinkedIn posts into clearer drafts that still sound like the person sending them.

What we are building

The product is intentionally focused: a reusable Voice Profile, mode-aware editing, visible changes, and checks for names, dates, numbers, links, and commitments. The final decision always stays with the writer.

What VoiceDraft does not promise

  • It does not promise to bypass AI detectors or make text undetectable.
  • It does not invent stories, credentials, evidence, or outcomes.
  • It does not guarantee engagement, replies, reach, or revenue.

Who it is for

VoiceDraft is designed for independent consultants, small agency owners, and other English-speaking professionals who use AI to get a first draft moving but want the final message to remain recognizably theirs.
