    FAQ

    Common questions about AI humanizing

    Is this an AI detector bypass tool?

    No. VoiceDraft is a professional editing tool and does not promise to bypass AI detection systems.

    What does the AI humanizer change?

    It targets generic phrasing, unnecessary framing, repetition, weak structure, and a mismatch with your selected Voice Profile.

    Can I use it without a Voice Profile?

    Yes. The default mode aims for clear, direct, warm professional English. A profile adds your own observable preferences.
